and things worth making.

Jake DevWorks · November 1, 2026

I’m Jake Ardoin, a software developer and maker in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Jake DevWorks brings together the work I do with software, websites, and physical design.

I help businesses and individuals bring their ideas to life—whether that means simplifying a business process, creating a website and building an online presence, or designing something you can hold. From the first conversation to the finished project, you’ll work directly with me.

Software that fits the job

My development work includes custom applications, APIs, automation, and AI tools. I work with clients to understand their requirements and turn those into working software, including the infrastructure and documentation that support it.

A home for your business online

A business website should make it easy to understand what you offer and how to contact you. My work also includes helping businesses establish their online presence through business listings and website SEO.

From a model to a physical piece

I also offer custom 3D design and printing for businesses, hobbies, and decoration. That can mean a practical accessory, a prototype, a display, or a personalized piece.

A useful starting point is a sketch, a photo, or an existing model, along with the dimensions and a description of how you intend to use the finished item.

What you’ll find here

This site is a place for project examples and notes from the workbench: software development, design decisions, printing experiments, and the details behind a build.

If you have a project in mind, tell me about it. We can work out what it needs and where to start.
