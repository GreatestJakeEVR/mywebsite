03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

A developer’s mindset.
A maker’s curiosity.

I’m Jake, a software developer and maker based in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and the founder of Jake DevWorks.

I build business websites, applications, APIs, and AI tools. My background also includes developing an RFID tracking application during an internship at NASA’s Stennis Space Center and teaching programming as a supplemental instructor at LSU.

Outside the code, I work with CAD, 3D printing, electronics, and small computing platforms. I enjoy figuring out how things work—and making something useful with that knowledge.
